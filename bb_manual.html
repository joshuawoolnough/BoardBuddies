WHAT'S IN THE BOX

Board Buddies TCG is a card game for 2–4 players built around the Board Buddies universe. You field a party of three units — Buddies, Wilds, and Rivals — and fight to knock out all of your opponent's units. Alternatively, team up against a shared Rival boss in Co-Op mode.

Every mechanic runs on cards. There are no dice and no separate tracking boards. Charge tokens are tracked with a whiteboard marker on the circles provided on the Quick Start Card or mat.

SUPPORT CARDS

Skill Cards — cost MP from the matching Buddy's pool. Only playable by the named Buddy. Each has a Burst Number used for BREAK checks.

Sketch Cards — cost Charges (1 or 2). The closest Sketch to the top of your deck when you have 3 Charges is the one you can play. After using a Sketch, reshuffle your support deck.

Item Cards — no cost. Any unit can use them. Each has a Burst Number.

Event Cards — carry keywords (Rush, Delay, Stall, Haste). Played from the support deck, take effect immediately.

STATUS CARDS

Status Cards are placed directly on top of the affected unit when triggered. They show the effect, duration, and cure. Discard when expired. Multiple statuses stack on the same unit's corner.

04
HOW TO PLAY

MISSION OBJECTIVE

You start with 3 friendly units — a mix of Buddies, Wilds, and Rivals. Use your Support Deck to modify stats, trigger BREAKs, use Sketches, and apply effects. First player to knock out all of the opponent's units wins.

Co-Op challenge: Set up 3 Rival units in the top Rival zone. You and 1–3 friends attack and defend together — take them all down to win.

ACTIONS PER BUDDY (WILD OR RIVAL) PER TURN

Each of your three units takes one action per turn. Players alternate full turns (all three units act). Choose one action per unit:

DRAW

Reveal the top card of your Support Deck. If the Burst total adds to 4, go to BREAK (step 4). If not, check the card and play its effect if you can.

ATTACK

Attack using your base ATK value, or add a Support Card boost. If you choose not to attack this turn, earn +1 Charge instead.

GUARD

Turn your unit card sideways and move it to the Guard Position slot (middle row). Incoming damage is reduced by this unit's DEF value.

BREAK

If the Burst total = 4 on a DRAW, your BREAK fires. If not, no BREAK occurs. Wilds and Rivals trigger their Power Surge instead.

CHARGE

If you have 3 Charges, you may play the Sketch Card closest to the top of your Support Deck. After using it, reshuffle your Support Deck.

VITALITY (HP)

Each unit's HP is divided into sections by Vitality Hearts shown on their card. When cumulative damage crosses a threshold, flip or discard the front Vitality section. A unit with no Vitality Hearts remaining is KO'd.

Revive a KO'd Buddy using a Sticky Tape Item Card (restores 1 Vitality Heart). Or use Blob Bubble for a complete party HP restoration.

SUPPORT DECK

Your Support Deck holds your Skill, Sketch, Item, and Event Cards. When the deck runs out, the Discard Pile becomes the new Support Deck. All cards in the Support Deck have a Burst Number (shown in a badge on the card) used during DRAW actions.

DUEL MODE (2 players)

Both players set up their Play Areas with 3 active units in the bottom row. Flip a coin — winner takes the first turn. Players alternate turns. First to KO all three opponent units wins.

CO-OP MODE (2–4 players)

Place up to 3 Rival Boss cards in the top Rival row of one shared Play Area. Players take turns together attacking the Rivals. Rival cards act at the end of each player's turn, using their printed stats. Defeat all Rivals to win together.

THE CHARGE SYSTEM

Charges fuel Sketch Cards — the most powerful moves in the game. Track charges using the whiteboard marker circles on your Quick Start Card or mat. Each unit can hold a maximum of 3 Charges.

EARNING CHARGES

SituationCharges earned
DRAW action occurs AND you play the drawn card+1 Charge
ATTACK is used AND modified with a Support Card+1 Charge
You choose NOT to attack this turn+1 Charge
You take a hit from a modified (boosted) attack+1 Charge

USING CHARGES — SKETCH CARDS

When a unit has 3 Charges, find the Sketch Card closest to the top of your Support Deck that belongs to that unit. Play it for its EFFECT, then reshuffle your entire Support Deck. Wipe all 3 charge circles for that unit.

If you DRAW a Sketch Card during a DRAW action but don't have 3 Charges, you simply keep your charges — the Sketch Card is discarded without being used, and no charges are spent.

THE BREAK SYSTEM

BREAK Abilities are the most powerful moves in the game. They fire automatically during a DRAW action when the Buddy's Burst + the drawn card's Burst = 4.

Each Buddy Card shows their Buddy Burst number on the featured skill panel. When a Buddy DRAWs a card and the two numbers add to exactly 4, BREAK fires instead of the card's normal effect.

FOCUS STATUS CARD

The Focus Status Card bypasses the Burst check entirely: the next DRAW action by this unit automatically triggers BREAK regardless of the actual Burst total.

DONNY'S CRITICAL HIT

Donny has no BREAK ability in Set 1. When his DRAW reveals a Burst total of 4 (Donny Burst #2 + Card Burst #2), he triggers a Critical Hit instead: 1.6× ATK to the target. Donny's Quack Attack Skill Card also notes this explicitly.

WILD AND RIVAL POWER SURGE

When a Wild or Rival unit's DRAW results in Burst total = 4, they fire their Power Surge instead of a BREAK:

  • Wild Power Surge — 2.5× ATK to one foe
  • Rival Power Surge — 3× ATK to one foe
